THE ARC

How far out a sector is, as 0 to 1: sector / SKY.arcSectors, clamped, so the trend arrives at sector and the sky holds there. Every setting below is a pair read at that fraction, and a sector wanders off the trend by hueWander.

THE ANCHOR, SECTOR BY SECTOR

Upper band A, lower band B. The wander is what makes the ramp jagged; the trend under it runs from blue to ember the short way round the wheel, through violet.

WHAT A SECTOR MAY ROLL

A kind held back by from cannot come up until the arc reaches it, and one weighted to nothing is out of the pool whatever its gate says. The share is the first pick of a sector: once the emissive cap is spent, every glowing kind drops out and the rest grow to fill the gap.

HOW BIG, AND HOW NEAR

A bigger world sits nearer and so parallaxes more, which is what depth buys. Only one giant at a time, since the grid planets are spread over is three cells wide.

THE WORLD KINDS

Every branch of the uType switch at the palette this sector would give it. Sharp, with the depth-of-field blur skipped; bloom still applies, since a glowing world is mostly what its glow does in the composite.

HOW THE NEBULA WORKS
