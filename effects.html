GEOMETRY II SCREEN EFFECTS

A LIVE FRAME

A real sector, simulated. The buttons fire the game's own triggers: a tear is glitchAt, the ripple is a ship arriving. Bloom is worked from the header; the rest of the post chain is constants in the shader.

WHAT THE COMPOSITE IS HOLDING

Rebuilt every frame from whatever is on screen, sorted by how much of the picture each one moves, and cut to what the shader can loop over.

THE POST CHAIN

Between the last thing drawn and the pixels on the glass. The scene lands in an offscreen target so that everything here can sample it more than once, and at coordinates other than the ones it was drawn at. Stages marked CRT need the filter on.

AND IN MORE DETAIL

THE DISTORTION BENCH

The composite pass over a ruled grid. Point the mouse at the canvas to move the source. Everything goes in through setLenses, setTears and setWarp, so a setting found here means the same thing on a weapon or a hull.

WHERE EACH ONE COMES FROM

A warp bends the picture continuously for as long as its owner is there; a wake lays that bend along a line behind something moving; a glitch tears it briefly where something landed.

SPACE BENT - HULLS

Radius is world units. A reach a little past the furthest point of the outline puts the heaviest part of the bend on the silhouette itself.

THE SCREEN SHAKEN

Shake moves the eye the world and background layers are projected from. The HUD pass is given a camera with none of it, so the frame lurches and the readouts hold still.
