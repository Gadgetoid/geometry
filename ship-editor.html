The design only, as config.js states it: what the shape and the numbers imply is left for the game to work out. What the shop fits to the player is the run's, so it is named against the hardpoint it goes on and not written here; startingSpecials is carried through as it was read. KEYS

V select / move   O outline point   H hardpoint   M seam   S scale selection   X delete
DRAG move node, empty space boxes a selection
TWO FINGERS or WHEEL pan   PINCH or CMD+wheel zoom   SHIFT+wheel pan sideways
MIDDLE or SPACE+drag pan   ESC clear selection
SHIFT add to the selection, by click or by box: a mirror seam, or several nodes to drag at once
ALT ignore snap while dragging   ARROWS nudge selection (SHIFT x5)
RIGHT CLICK delete node   DEL delete selection   F fit
CTRL+Z / CTRL+SHIFT+Z undo / redo
